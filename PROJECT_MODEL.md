# SimTrail project model

SimTrail follows an **open-source core with official commercial services**
model.

## Open-source community project

The SimTrail source code published in this repository is licensed under the
Apache License 2.0. Individuals and organizations may use, modify, distribute,
and build commercial products from that code under the terms of the license.

The public project will continue to provide a useful, independently usable
simulation project manager. Bug fixes, the local run register, public data
formats, and the solver-neutral adapter foundation are not trial features.

Code already released under Apache-2.0 remains available under that license.
A later business-model change cannot revoke rights already granted for a
published version.

## SimTrail brand

The source-code license does not grant rights to present a modified product as
an official SimTrail release. The SimTrail name, logo, and official-release
identity are governed by [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md).

Community forks are welcome, but substantially modified distributions should
use their own product name and may accurately state that they are based on
SimTrail.

## Official paid services

The maintainers may offer paid services without changing the open-source
license of this repository, including:

- installation, migration, and integration assistance;
- priority support and compatibility commitments;
- training and engineering workflow consulting;
- certified or signed release packages; and
- custom adapter and connector development.

Paying for official support is optional and is not required to use or modify
the open-source project.

## Future enterprise capabilities

Future enterprise offerings may include team administration, SSO, centralized
deployment, managed synchronization, audit controls, and contractual support.
Their source availability and license will be stated clearly before release.

Enterprise development must not retroactively change the license of code
already published here or deliberately make the community application
nonfunctional. Shared protocols and data formats should remain documented so
the community version is not locked to an official hosted service.

## Contributions

Contributions accepted into this repository are licensed under Apache-2.0 as
described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Contributors do not transfer
ownership of their copyright merely by submitting a contribution.
