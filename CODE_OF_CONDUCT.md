# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

SimTrail contributors and maintainers commit to a respectful, inclusive, and
professional community. Participation must be free from harassment regardless
of experience, education, disability, appearance, nationality, ethnicity,
gender identity, sexual orientation, religion, or other personal attributes.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and engineering safety boundaries.
- Credit other people's work.
- Accept moderation decisions and correct harmful behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, publishing confidential engineering data, and sustained
disruption are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing SimTrail.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the current repository maintainers. When
no private project contact is published, use GitHub's private vulnerability
reporting channel and prefix the report title with `CODE OF CONDUCT`.
Maintainers may remove content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban participants. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as reasonably possible.
